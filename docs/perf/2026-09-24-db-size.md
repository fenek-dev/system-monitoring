# History DB size — projection (final review R-I2 / R-I3, 2026-09-24)

Rulings: the file must stay under 200 MB even with the dashboard open all day; the in-memory fallback targets < 10 MB.
Mechanisms (MonitorStore):

- **Rollup fold:** 1 m and 15 m buckets keep only apps whose bucket average passes a `RecordConfig` threshold
  (CPU ≥ 0.5 %, any GPU, net ≥ 1 KB/s, disk ≥ 100 KB/s, memory ≥ 200 MB). The rest fold into `other`.
- **Size guard:** after each maintenance pass, if `page_count × page_size` > 180 MB, the oldest raw rows are deleted
  in steps until the used pages fit 90 % of the cap (162 MB). Raw from the last hour is never deleted, and raw that old
  is already rolled up to 1 m. The prune is logged (`MonitorStore`, error level). Ranges that start before the oldest
  raw row read 1 m rollups plus the raw tail, so they show no gap.
- **WAL:** `journal_size_limit` = 8 MB, and quit runs `wal_checkpoint(TRUNCATE)`.

## Row sizes (measured)

Measured with `dbstat` on the real dev DB (`agent-abd1d67d19bf092e2`, 3,943 raw samples, about 3 h of use). Each size
includes the row's `(app_id, ts)` index.

| Level | System row | App row | Apps per sample/bucket, before fold | Apps per sample/bucket, after fold |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| raw | 290 B | 66.6 B | 28 | 28 (the engine already folds per sample) |
| 1 m | 387 B | 74.6 B | 68 | 31 + `other` (30.7 qualifying on average) |
| 15 m | ~390 B | 83 B | 143 | 35 + `other` (35.3 qualifying on average) |

## File store, steady state

| Component | UI closed (5 s raw) | Dashboard open all day (1 s raw) |
|---|---|---|
| Raw, 24 h | 17,280 × 2.16 KB = **37 MB** | 86,400 × 2.16 KB = 186 MB → the guard keeps ~16 h of raw (~124 MB) |
| 1 m, 7 d | 10,080 × 2.77 KB = **28 MB** (55 MB before the fold) | 28 MB |
| 15 m, 30 d | 2,880 × 3.38 KB = **10 MB** (35 MB before the fold) | 10 MB |
| **Total** | **~75 MB** | **≤ 180 MB** file (224 MB without the guard) |

The WAL comes on top: at most 8 MB between checkpoints, and 0 after a clean quit. The earlier "< 60 MB for 30 days"
(soak report) counted neither the rollup app rows nor a UI left open.

## In-memory fallback (file store failed to open)

Retention: raw 1 h, 1 m for 24 h, 15 m for 7 d. Guard: 10 MB cap, never pruning the last 15 min of raw. The numbers
below come from `SizeTests.inMemoryFallbackStaysUnderTenMegabytes`, a full synthetic steady state built with the row
shapes above (28 apps per sample, 32 per 1 m bucket, 36 per 15 m bucket):

| Scenario | Before maintenance | After maintenance |
|---|---|---|
| UI closed (5 s raw) | 6.8 MB | **6.8 MB** |
| Dashboard open all hour (1 s raw) | 12.9 MB | **9.0 MB** (the guard pruned raw older than ~30 min) |
